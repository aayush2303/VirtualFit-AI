ABOUT VIRTUALFIT AI

The future of fashion is virtual.

VirtualFit AI is an AI-powered virtual fashion try-on system designed to help users visualize clothing on themselves before making a fashion decision.

THE PROBLEM

Shopping should feel more personal.

Online fashion shopping often makes it difficult for users to understand how a garment will look on their own body. Traditional product photographs cannot represent every individual's appearance, proportions and style.

OUR VISION

Making fashion more visual.

VirtualFit AI explores how artificial intelligence and computer vision can create a more interactive fashion experience by allowing users to visualize garments through virtual try-on technology.

AI Powered

Uses artificial intelligence concepts to analyze images and generate personalized virtual fashion experiences.

User Focused

Designed around a simple workflow that allows users to upload, select, generate and review their looks.

Privacy Conscious

The platform is designed with user image privacy and responsible handling of visual data in mind.

Built as an AI & Computer Science Project

VirtualFit AI demonstrates the application of modern artificial intelligence, computer vision, web development and user interface design concepts in a practical fashion technology use case.
