From photo to perfect fit.

VirtualFit AI combines computer vision, garment analysis and generative AI to create a personalized virtual fashion experience.

STEP 01

Upload Your Photo

Upload a clear full-body or upper-body photograph. The system analyzes the image to understand the person's pose, body structure and visible features.

STEP 02

Select a Garment

Choose an outfit from your digital wardrobe. Garment information such as shape, category, texture and visual appearance is processed by the system.

STEP 03

AI Body & Garment Analysis

Computer vision techniques identify important visual features and estimate how the selected garment should align with the user's pose and body structure.

STEP 04

Virtual Try-On Generation

The AI generates a new visual representation where the selected garment appears naturally integrated with the uploaded person's image.

STEP 05

Review & Save Your Look

Review the generated result, save it to your history, add it to favorites, download it or share your look.
